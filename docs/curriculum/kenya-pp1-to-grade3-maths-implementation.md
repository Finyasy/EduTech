# Kenya PP1 To Grade 3 Maths Curriculum Implementation

## Purpose

This package turns the PP1 Term 2 scheme into review-ready curriculum artifacts for LearnBridge. It starts with PP1 Mathematical Activities and keeps the structure extensible for PP2, Grade 1, Grade 2, and Grade 3.

The implementation is documentation-first. It is intended for curriculum review before any product, database, or classroom workflow is coded.

## Source Alignment

Primary sources to use as the curriculum authority:

- KICD PP1 Curriculum Design: https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/PP-1-Curriculum-Designs-Dec-2017C-1-min.pdf
- KICD Pre-Primary Curriculum Designs page: https://kicd.ac.ke/cbc-materials/pre-primary/
- KICD Revised Grade 1-3 Mathematics Design: https://kicd.ac.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Grade-1-3-Mathematics-Revised.pdf

Implementation rules derived from those sources:

- Keep the learning area as Mathematical Activities.
- Use 5 lessons per week for PP1 through Grade 3 maths planning.
- Make activities competency-based, play-based, inquiry-based, and observable.
- Link learning to core competencies, values, PCIs, and differentiated assessment.
- Use locally available materials before digital activities.
- Treat KICD designs as the source of truth where a school scheme is repetitive or misaligned.

## PP1 Lesson Product Pattern

Every PP1 maths lesson should use this repeatable structure:

| Step | Purpose | Example evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up song or rhyme | Set language, rhythm, and confidence | Learner joins counting or comparison song |
| Concrete object activity | Anchor concept in real objects | Learner counts, sorts, lifts, fills, traces, or matches objects |
| Pair or group exploration | Build collaboration and language | Learner explains choice to peer or takes turns |
| Teacher observation prompt | Capture competency evidence | Teacher records what learner can do independently |
| Learner performance task | Confirm mastery through action | Learner completes a small task, not just an oral response |
| Home connection | Extend learning using household materials | Learner identifies cups, tins, routines, or comparison examples at home |

## PP1 Strand Model

| Strand | Sub-strands | Product interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classification | Sorting, grouping, matching, pairing | Pre-number reasoning: same/different, one attribute, two attributes, why items belong together |
| Numbers | Recognition, counting concrete objects, sequencing, number writing, number puzzles | Quantity-to-symbol progression: object count, spoken number, sequence, missing number, written numeral |
| Measurement | Long/short, heavy/light, capacity, time/daily routine, money readiness | Practical comparison before formal units: compare, sort, order, explain |

## PP1 To PP2 Progression Matrix

| Competency area | PP1 expectation | PP2 extension | Grade 1 readiness check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classification | Sort or match objects by one visible attribute | Sort by more than one attribute and explain the rule | Groups objects accurately and states the grouping reason |
| Counting | Count concrete objects in small sets, including 5-9 in Term 2 | Count larger sets with fewer prompts | Counts objects accurately without skipping or double-counting |
| Number recognition | Identify number symbols 1-9 | Recognize and use a wider number range | Matches a numeral to a concrete quantity |
| Sequencing | Order numbers 1-9 and fill simple missing numbers | Order longer sequences and describe before/after | Places numbers in order and names what comes before or after |
| Number writing | Model, trace, join, color, write, and type early numerals | Write more numerals with improved formation and independence | Forms numerals legibly enough for teacher recognition |
| Length | Compare long and short sides of familiar objects | Order more than two objects by length | Uses longer/shorter correctly in context |
| Mass | Compare heavy and light through lifting and sorting | Compare more objects and justify choices | Identifies heavier/lighter using safe classroom objects |
| Capacity | Fill, empty, compare, and order containers | Estimate which holds more and verify by filling | Uses full/empty, more/less, big/small appropriately |
| Time | Sequence daily routines | Use broader routine language and simple calendar references | Orders morning, school, home, and evening activities |

## PP1 To Grade 3 Competency Ladder

| Level | Numbers | Measurement | Geometry and space | Data/algebra readiness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PP1 | Concrete counting, number symbols 1-9, sequencing, number writing readiness | Long/short, heavy/light, capacity, daily routine | Informal shape, side, and object comparison language | Sorting, grouping, matching, pairing |
| PP2 | Broader number range, stronger numeral writing, before/after, more/less | Practical measurement comparison with more independence | More confident shape and position language | Pattern, grouping rule, simple tally readiness |
| Grade 1 | Whole numbers, addition and subtraction readiness, early operations | Length, mass, capacity, time, money in practical contexts | Lines, shapes, spatial relationships | Sorting information and early pattern reasoning |
| Grade 2 | Deeper number concepts, operations, fractions readiness | Standard and non-standard measurement, money expansion | Lines, shapes, position, direction | Organizing simple information and identifying rules |
| Grade 3 | Stronger operations, fractions, problem solving | Metres, addition/subtraction of measures, money, time | Position, direction, geometry for upper primary transition | Data handling and algebraic thinking readiness |

## Review Workflow

1. Review `pp1-term-2-maths-map.md` for CBC/CBE alignment and local classroom realism.
2. Review `pp1-maths-assessment-bank.md` for observability and rubric clarity.
3. Add Term 1 and Term 3 schemes when available, using the same fields and lesson pattern.
4. Validate PP1 year coverage against the KICD PP1 design before extending into PP2.
5. Validate Grade 1-3 progression against the KICD revised Grade 1-3 mathematics design before building lower-primary product flows.

## Reviewer Checklist

| Question | Expected answer |
| --- | --- |
| Is every lesson CBC/CBE aligned? | Yes, every lesson has an inquiry question, activity, resource, and observable evidence. |
| Is every activity observable? | Yes, each learner must count, sort, match, trace, lift, fill, order, sequence, or explain. |
| Is every assessment tied to a competency? | Yes, oral responses support observation but do not replace performance evidence. |
| Are local materials realistic? | Yes, household and classroom materials are used before specialized resources. |
| Are digital activities supportive? | Yes, digital tracing, matching, sequencing, and photo/audio notes support hands-on learning. |
| Does the sequence prepare learners for the next level? | Yes, PP1 builds classification, number sense, writing readiness, and practical measurement for PP2 and Grade 1. |
